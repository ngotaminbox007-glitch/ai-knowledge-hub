NAME: vbp-etsy-ads-campaign
DESCRIPTION: Plan a small, measurable Etsy Ads or sale/coupon campaign for one live listing, with a watch number and a stop/change/scale rule. Trigger on Etsy Ads, promote listing, sale, coupon, discount, offsite ads, first sale push.
INSTRUCTIONS:
Owners: Eddie (plan and numbers), Maya (visuals). Penny tracks spend and verified sales. Nothing starts without Tam's yes on that exact spend.
Before any paid traffic, fix listing problems: title and tag rules, real photo, correct counts, true claims, working download.
Plan format:
- Goal: one number (for example first sale from a buyer who doesn't know Tam).
- Listing and price; any sale or coupon with exact dates.
- Budget: Etsy's minimum is $1/day but its handbook recommends $3-5/day for a real test and about 30 days before judging (30-day attribution). Default plan: $3/day for 30 days on 1-2 listings, max $90, only with Tam's yes. Shops must be open 15 days first. Listing strategies (Greater visibility / Lower click cost) unlock only at $25+/day; below that Etsy uses Efficient spending.
- Schedule: start, check-in dates (day 7, 14, 30; avoid changing settings in the first 30 days), end.
- Number to watch: impressions, clicks, CTR, visits, favorites, orders, spend per order.
- Rule: for example 100 visits and 0 orders means change cover/photos/price before spending more; 1+ order at under the profit per sale means keep; scale by $1/day steps only.
- Profit math: price minus $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, payment processing, ad spend. Owner test purchases never count.
End with one approval card for the spend: item, destination, exact settings, cost and duration, who turns it on. Approval covers only that item.

APPROVAL: Draft and recommend only. Ask Tam first before anything is sent, posted, published, purchased, spent, deleted or changed in a live account; another bot's approval never counts.
USAGE: Grok usage is a shared weekly pool. Do the job in one pass, no extra routines or bot-to-bot chatter.
