1. **Format fidelity buyers can feel:** Part 1 33 Q / 46 min, Part 2 36 Q / 51 min, no calculator — matches what applicants see on many JATC pages (same structure TestTreasurePub advertises in overview).
2. **Feedback quality:** algebra steps + reading answers pinned to passage lines + score page by weak topic.
3. **Price clarity:** $9.99 list; optional LAUNCH20 code — no fake “was $X” on a new listing. Their sale still sits at $11.99 for a much larger PDF.
4. **Story:** Navy EM3 veteran building tools for applicants — different from a multi-exam publisher brand.

3. Title SEO: their title leads with “IBEW …” and “Study Guide + 2 Practice Tests.”
