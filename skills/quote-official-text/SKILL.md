# quote-official-text

A summary can quietly change what a source means, and the next reader treats it as fact.

## The rule
1. Quote the words exactly as they appear, and give the date and time of the source.
2. Put your own reading on a separate line labeled **Reading:**, never mixed into the quote.
3. If two sources disagree, or a source is missing, say so. Don't pick one and move on.
4. Keep names, account numbers, medical details and other personal information out of the quote. Use a short excerpt that is safe to share, or keep the full quote in the private record and say only that a source was checked.

## Format
```
Source:  <who sent it>, <date and time>
Quote:   "<exact words>"
Reading: <your interpretation, clearly yours>
Conflicts / gaps: <none, or what disagrees or is missing>
```

Follows `core-guardrails` (last 4 digits only, never full numbers).
