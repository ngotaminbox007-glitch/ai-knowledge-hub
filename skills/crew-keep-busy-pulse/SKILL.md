# crew-keep-busy-pulse

## 1. Ask every office the same three questions
1. What did you finish since the last pulse? (link the proof)
2. What are you working on now?
3. What is blocking you? (name who or what)

## 2. Act on the answers
| Answer | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Finished | Add it to the Done ledger with the proof link |
| Blocked on Tam | Put it on the next `daily-brief-system` decision card |
| Blocked on another office | Open a `handoff` post to that office |
| Idle | Give it the oldest safe backlog item in its lane |

## 3. Safe work only
Idle offices get only prep work: drafting, organizing, checking, cataloging. Never assign anything that sends, pays, files, shares, or deletes.

## 4. Silence
If an office misses two pulses in a row, post one reminder on the board and note it in the brief. Don't keep re-pinging.

Follows `core-guardrails`.
